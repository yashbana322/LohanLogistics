Frequently Asked Freight Questions

Clear answers on rates, customs clearance, transit times, and multimodal container positioning.

How quickly can you position an empty container at our factory?

Within 6 to 12 hours across any of our 8 regional hub corridors. Every container is pre-inspected for seaworthiness, moisture seal integrity, and CSC compliance before dispatch.

Do you handle end-to-end customs clearance at ports?

Yes. Our licensed in-house customs brokerage manages ICEGATE submissions, duty tariff assessments, phytosanitary certificates, and green-channel clearances with zero handover delays.

How does your dedicated rail rake service compare with road haulage?

Dedicated block trains are 25% to 35% more cost-effective over distances exceeding 600 km, bypass highway toll congestion, and offer fixed, guaranteed departure windows.

Can we monitor cold-chain temperatures in real time?

Yes. All reefer containers feature calibrated temperature dataloggers that transmit live telemetry every 5 minutes with automated threshold alerts for pharmaceutical and perishable cargo.

What cargo insurance coverage is included?

All shipments are protected under standard Multimodal Transport Operator (MTO) liabilities, with optional comprehensive all-risk marine cargo coverage available directly upon quote acceptance.
